## Tally verification and limitation
Read current form LDDoxJ using the Tally connector on 18 November. One shared form already supports both intents. Required: intent, name, email, short request. Organisation, start/location/scope and profile URL are optional. No hidden fields or prefill defaults exist. No Tally settings/content were changed or published. To avoid silently dropping context or claiming an unverified integration, the website explicitly asks visitors to select intent and mention their topic in the form; it does not pass prefill parameters to Tally. Automatic preselection requires separately implementing and validating the form mapping.

Self notification is configured to silverback@wescaleit.com; a separate respondent-notification setting points to office@wescaleit.com with all answers. This was observed, not altered or delivery-tested. No real test lead was sent, no GA4 production receipt verified, no lift claimed. 50% is not a measured or guaranteed result.
